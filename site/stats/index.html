Counts only. No cookies, names, email addresses or IP addresses are stored. A visitor is recognized only within one day (UTC), through a daily key that is deleted when the day ends, so the same person on two days counts twice and cannot be followed across days.

Locations are looked up from the visitor's network address in the open DB-IP Lite city database (CC BY 4.0) and are approximate, often the nearest large city or the location of a company network or VPN. The address itself is never stored. Crawlers and link previews are not counted. Days run on UTC.
